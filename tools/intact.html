v0.2 · no API · runs in your browser

Semantic integrity check between a source text and its condensed or summarised version. A compression is allowed to drop a piece of information; it is never allowed to keep it while altering it: a rewritten value, a vanished negation, a corrupted identifier. All of that is mechanically checkable, right here in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere.
