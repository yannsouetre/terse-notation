TERSE FetchBench

The full MAP+FOCUS contract as a two-step agent: call 1 sees only the MAP and the question and replies with fetch addresses; the harness loads them (region fetches include their radius-1 intra-document references); call 2 answers from MAP + fetched content. Two granularities compared on the same 24 questions: B · doc-fetch (doc-level map, 4.5k chars; fetch = whole document L1) vs C · region-fetch (region map, 171 lines / 16.4k chars; fetch = regions + radius-1 closure, cap 8) vs D · two-stage fetch (route on the doc map, then on the elected document's own region map — fine routing at near-doc-map cost; the v0.1 finding motivating it: the region map's 16.4k chars ride every call, and region lines scored out of their document's context can mislead). Metrics: routing hit rate (deterministic), answer accuracy (judge), input tokens per question. The 12 formerly out-of-focus questions are the hard-routing cohort — including the two that resisted every doc-level map (F3/F4). B/C: 3 calls per question; D: 4. A D-only run \u2248 96 calls.
